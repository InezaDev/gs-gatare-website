About Our School

Excellence Rooted in Discipline

Groupe Scolaire Gatare TSS is a premier educational institution located in Gashasha Cell, Gatare Sector, Nyamagabe District, Rwanda. We are committed to nurturing academically strong, technically skilled, and morally upright citizens who contribute meaningfully to national development.

Our Mission

To provide quality, competence-based education that integrates academic excellence with practical TVET skills, producing graduates ready for the labor market, higher education, and entrepreneurship.

Our Vision

To become the leading TVET and academic center of excellence in the Southern Province, recognized for discipline, innovation, and graduate employability.

Our Values

Discipline · Integrity · Excellence · Respect · Innovation · Community Service. These values guide every learner, teacher, and staff member at G.S. Gatare TSS.

Our Motto
"Discipline Leads to Success"

Since our founding, this motto has anchored every decision, every classroom, and every graduate of G.S. Gatare TSS.

Academic Structure

Seven Pathways to Success

From early childhood through advanced TVET, G.S. Gatare TSS offers a complete educational journey under one disciplined roof.

01

Pre-Primary

Nursery & Kindergarten — foundational learning through play, discovery, and character formation.

02

Primary

P1 – P6 — Competence-based curriculum building literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking.

03

O'Level

S1 – S3 — Junior secondary education preparing learners for senior pathways.

04

A'Level AH

Arts & Humanities — History, Literature, Geography, and Languages for future leaders.

05

A'Level MS1

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry & Biology — preparing future scientists and engineers.

06

TVET AGR

Agriculture (Level 3–4) — hands-on crop and animal production, agribusiness, and modern farming.

07

TVET ANH

Animal Health (Level 3–4) — veterinary assistance, livestock management, and animal welfare.

Programs Showcase

Explore Our Trades

Industry-relevant TVET programs designed with employers, aligned to national competency standards, and delivered by experienced instructors.

TVET · Level 3 & 4

Agriculture (AGR)

Feed the nation. Grow the future.

The Agriculture program equips learners with modern crop production, livestock husbandry, soil management, irrigation, and agribusiness skills. Learners work on real school farms and partner cooperatives across Nyamagabe District.

  • Crop Production & Soil Science
  • Animal Husbandry & Nutrition
  • Farm Business Management
  • Practical Field Attachment
Apply for AGR
TVET · Level 3 & 4

Animal Health (ANH)

Care for animals. Protect livelihoods.

The Animal Health program trains veterinary assistants and livestock health workers. Learners master disease identification, treatment assistance, vaccination campaigns, and farm biosecurity — skills that are in high demand across rural and urban Rwanda.

  • Animal Anatomy & Physiology
  • Disease Diagnosis & Treatment Support
  • Vaccination & Biosecurity
  • Clinical Attachment with Vets
Apply for ANH
