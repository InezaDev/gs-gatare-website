About the School

An educational journey grounded in discipline.

G.S. Gatare TSS is a government-aided day school in the Southern Province of Rwanda, guiding learners from their earliest years through to technical excellence.

Our History

The history of G.S. Gatare TSS is maintained by the school administration. Verified historical documentation will be published here as it is officially released.

Our Mission

To provide inclusive, disciplined and practical education that prepares every learner for further study, dignified work and responsible citizenship — from Pre-Primary to TVET.

Core Purpose

Our Vision

A flagship technical secondary school in Nyamagabe where academic rigour and hands-on agricultural and veterinary training produce confident, employable graduates.

Academic Programs

A continuous path from early years to technical mastery.

Every stage is aligned to the Rwanda Basic Education Board curriculum, with accredited TVET pathways in Agriculture and Animal Health.

Pre-Primary (PPR)

Foundational literacy, numeracy and social-emotional development for the youngest learners.

Early Childhood

Primary (PR)

Competence-based primary education following the REB curriculum across all core subjects.

Basic EducationREB

Ordinary Level (O'Level)

Lower secondary education with strong foundations across sciences, humanities and languages.

Lower Secondary

Arts and Humanities (AH)

Advanced Level pathway in the humanities — literature, history, geography, languages — preparing learners for university and public service.

Advanced LevelHumanities

Mathematics & Science Stream One (MS1)

Advanced Level pathway in mathematics and physical sciences — a rigorous route to engineering, medicine and research.

Advanced LevelSciences
Technical Secondary Education

Accredited TVET trades at Levels 3, 4 and 5.

Practical, industry-aligned training in agriculture and animal health — delivered in workshops, laboratories and live farms.

Agriculture (AGR)

Crop production, soil management, agricultural technology, agribusiness and sustainable farming practices.

TVET L3–L5Crop ScienceAgribusiness

Animal Health (ANH)

Livestock management, animal health, veterinary assistance and animal husbandry — with supervised practicals.

TVET L3–L5VeterinaryHusbandry

ANH Level 3 Modules

Ruminant Farming · Concentrate Feeds · Fodder Crops Conservation · Domestic Animal Description · Fodder Growing & Pasture · Minor Surgery · Disease Prevention & Diagnosis · Livestock Health Recording.

NESA StandardLevel 3

ANH Level 4 & 5 Modules

Animal Physiology · Infectious & Parasitic Diseases · Pig, Rabbit & Poultry Farming · Milking Parlor Operations · Livestock Extension · Major Surgery · Necropsy · Reproductive Technology · Veterinary Ethics.

NESA StandardLevel 4Level 5
