About the School

A community built on discipline, purpose & skill.

Groupe Scolaire Gatare TSS is a modern integrated learning institution located in Gashasha Cell, Gatare Sector, Nyamagabe District, Rwanda. From Pre-Primary through A'Level and TVET, we prepare learners for real opportunity — academic, professional and entrepreneurial.

Holistic curriculumCompetency-based learning aligned with RBC & REB standards.
TVET-first mindsetHands-on trades in Agriculture & Animal Husbandry.
Digital transparencyLive portals for students, parents, teachers & leaders.

Programs Showcase

Trade-ready skills, employer-ready graduates.

Our TVET flagship programs combine theory, practical labs and industry exposure.

Agriculture (AGR)

Level 3 & 4 · Crop production, horticulture, soil fertility and farm management.

  • Practical farm labs
  • Agri-business training
  • Cooperative placement

Animal Husbandry (ANH)

Level 3 & 4 · Livestock care, breeding, animal health and dairy production.

  • On-campus livestock unit
  • Veterinary fundamentals
  • Dairy value chain
