WHO WE ARE

Learning with purpose.
Growing with discipline.

G.S. GATARE TSSKnowledge into action.

Groupe Scolaire Gatare is a Government-Aided Day School serving learners, families and the wider community in Gatare Sector, Nyamagabe District.

Our school profile brings together general education and technical learning. Through classroom study and practical experiences, learners are encouraged to develop knowledge, responsibility and skills that can contribute to their communities.

OUR MISSION

To support learners in building knowledge, discipline and useful practical skills for lifelong learning.

OUR VISION

To nurture responsible, capable learners who contribute positively to society.

Admissions & inquiries

Parents and prospective learners are welcome to contact the school for current admission requirements, available places and important dates.

Admission pathways

G.S. Gatare welcomes inquiries for day-student pathways across the following levels:

  • Pre-Primary (PPR)
  • Primary (PR)
  • Ordinary Level (OL)
  • Advanced Level — AH and MS1
  • Technical Secondary — AGR and ANH (L3–L5)
Please note

Admission conditions, required documents, fees (if applicable), vacancies and deadlines may change. Contact the school directly to confirm the current official requirements before applying.
